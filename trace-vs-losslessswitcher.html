Audex Trace vs LosslessSwitcher: what's different.

LosslessSwitcher is a useful free tool for automatic sample-rate switching. Trace is for Apple Music listeners who also want to see what is playing, whether the output matches, and what is coming up next.

Start here

If automatic switching is all you need, use LosslessSwitcher.

LosslessSwitcher is a useful tool. It is open source, free, and does the one thing most people want: it switches your DAC's sample rate to match what Apple Music is playing. If automatic switching is all you need, use LosslessSwitcher. You do not need to pay for that.

Core difference

Trace is built around visibility.

LosslessSwitcher is built around automatic sample rate switching. Trace is built around visibility: the codec, sample rate, and bit depth of what is playing, whether your output matches it, and what is coming up next. It switches too.

Accuracy

Check your own Mac before paying.

Readout accuracy depends on your setup. Detection behavior varies across DACs, interfaces, and configurations.

That is why Trace ships free Playback Verification. Download it, play a few tracks, and compare what Trace reports against your DAC's own display. If the numbers match on your Mac with your hardware, buy the full app with confidence. If they do not, you do not need to pay. You verify on your own equipment before spending anything.
