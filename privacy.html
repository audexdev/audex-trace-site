Privacy Policy

Audex Trace is built as a local-first Apple Music companion. This policy explains what the app and related services process for purchase, license, support, and update workflows.

Local playback data

Playback format detection runs locally on your Mac using Music.app state and local playback metadata/log signals. Audex Trace does not upload your listening history for audio-quality detection.

  • Audex Trace does not modify, enhance, record, or upload your audio.
  • Audex Trace does not require Apple Music account sign-in or cloud library API access.
  • Artwork fallback may send track, artist, or album search terms to the Apple iTunes Search API when local artwork sources miss.

Purchase and license data

For purchase, activation, recovery, support, and refund handling, Audex may store the purchase email address, Stripe checkout/payment identifiers, license status, encrypted license key data, hashed license key data, and related timestamps.

License verification may contact Audex Trace servers to check whether a license is active and to issue a signed entitlement token for the app.

Network services

Audex Trace and related web services may use these third-party services:

  • Stripe for checkout and payment processing.
  • Resend for activation and recovery email delivery.
  • GitHub Releases and Sparkle update feeds for app distribution and update checks.
  • Apple iTunes Search API for artwork fallback when local artwork sources miss.

Analytics

No analytics are used in the initial public release. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before they are enabled.

Retention

Purchase, license, and support records may be kept for as long as needed for activation, recovery, refunds, accounting, fraud prevention, legal obligations, and support history.

Contact

For privacy, license, or data requests, contact trace@audex.dev.
