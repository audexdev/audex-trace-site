Privacy

Built for your Mac, not for tracking.

Playback format detection runs locally on your Mac using Music.app state and local playback metadata/log signals.

Audex Trace does not modify, enhance, record, or upload your audio.

No listening history is uploaded for audio-quality detection.

No Apple Music account sign-in or cloud library API access.

License verification, update checks, purchase checkout, activation email delivery, recovery email delivery, and artwork fallback may use the network.

For purchase and license operations, Audex stores the purchase email, Stripe checkout/payment identifiers, license status, encrypted license key data, hashed license key data, and related timestamps needed for activation, recovery, support, and refunds.

Artwork fallback may send track, artist, or album search terms to the iTunes Search API when local artwork sources miss.

No analytics for the initial beta.
