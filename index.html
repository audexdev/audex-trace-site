The gap

Music changes. macOS output often does not.

Apple Music can move between 44.1, 48, 96, and 192 kHz while the selected macOS output format stays fixed.

Manual switching in Audio MIDI Setup interrupts listening, and Apple Music’s own UI keeps technical playback details out of sight.

Solution

A focused companion for Apple Music playback.

Keep the details Apple Music usually hides close by: the active playback format, the next few tracks, and early signals when quality or playback may not behave as expected.

PN

Playing Next Queue

Open a compact queue view and see up to 3 upcoming Apple Music tracks without leaving the player.

EQ

Estimated Queue Quality

Preview estimated Lossless or Hi-Res availability for queued tracks before playback reaches them.

SK

Skip Risk Detection

Flag upcoming tracks that look likely to fail or skip, so playback issues are visible earlier.

AR

Actual Playback Readout

Keep the current codec, bit depth, sample rate, and output match status visible while you listen.
