Support

Support

For purchase, license, compatibility, and playback-format detection issues, contact Audex Trace support.

Contact

Email trace@audex.dev. Include the purchase email when the issue involves activation, recovery, or refunds.

License recovery

Use the activation email or the license recovery flow when available. If recovery fails, contact support with the purchase email and approximate purchase date.

Compatibility or detection issues

Before contacting support, run the free Compatibility Check in Audex Trace. If the issue continues, include:

  • macOS version.
  • Audex Trace version.
  • Output device name.
  • Whether Apple Music is playing locally on the Mac.
  • A short description of what failed, such as format detection, artwork, output match status, or sample-rate matching.

Refund requests

Compatibility refund requests are handled under the Refund Policy. Contact support within 7 days of purchase.
