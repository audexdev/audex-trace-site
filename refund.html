Refunds

Refund Policy

Audex Trace offers a narrow compatibility refund for the first public release. Use the free compatibility check before purchase whenever possible.

7-day compatibility refund

If Audex Trace cannot detect Apple Music playback format on a supported macOS version, contact support within 7 days of purchase.

To qualify, the issue must occur with local Apple Music playback on a supported macOS version and must not be caused by an unsupported player, unsupported macOS version, unavailable output device capability, or a device that does not expose compatible macOS sample rates.

Before requesting a refund

  • Run the free Compatibility Check inside Audex Trace.
  • Confirm Apple Music is installed and playing locally on the Mac.
  • Confirm the Mac is running macOS 15 or later.
  • Contact trace@audex.dev with the purchase email, macOS version, output device, and a brief description of the failure.

Refund handling

Compatibility refunds are handled as full refunds. Partial refunds are not used for v1 compatibility refunds.

After a refund, the license may be revoked or marked inactive. Revocation is enforced on the next successful online verification. If the app is offline, Pro access may continue until the signed entitlement token expires, currently up to 3 days.

Non-compatibility cases

Audex Trace is a one-time purchase and does not include a general no-questions-asked trial. Refunds for reasons outside compatibility are handled case by case where required by law or payment processor rules.
